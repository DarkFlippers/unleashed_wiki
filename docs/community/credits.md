# Credits & thanks

This wiki is a compilation of other people's work. Nothing here would exist without the firmware developers who
build Unleashed, the researchers who reverse-engineer the systems around us, and the writers who explain it all.
Individual pages carry their own credits block pointing at the exact originals.

## @gromovyna

A huge thank-you goes to **[@gromovyna](https://t.me/gromovyna)**. His articles are the single most valuable body
of material behind this wiki: hands-on write-ups of real access-control research — Iron Logic, Vizit, Moskvyonok,
MIFARE Classic magic tags, the Nested and Fudan backdoors, LF RFID blanks and more. Where this wiki goes into
practical depth on NFC and access systems, it is because he published that depth first.

Every page below is built from one of his articles. Please read the originals and subscribe to his channel.

## Flipper Devices

Thanks to **[Flipper Devices](https://flipperzero.one)** for creating the Flipper Zero and for maintaining the
official documentation at **[docs.flipper.net](https://docs.flipper.net/zero)**. The hardware facts, background
concepts and much of the stock-firmware behaviour described in this wiki come from their docs and their open-source
firmware. This wiki is an independent community project and is **not affiliated with Flipper Devices**.
